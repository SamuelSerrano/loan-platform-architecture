# Initial Contract Catalog

**Status:** Approved documentary allowlist for initial M1 scope

**Date:** 2026-08-18

## 1. Purpose and interpretation

This catalog selects the minimum contracts for the first walking skeleton and approves their fields under `Q-004`. It is a documentary registry, not OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, JSON Schema, code, or evidence that a contract is published. Anything not listed is denied.

The classifications are exactly `Public`, `Internal`, `Confidential`, `Restricted`, and `Restricted secret`. No initial field is approved as `Restricted secret`. `Service` means only owner-controlled service APIs/queues and encrypted stores; `edge` means the authenticated public API boundary; `applicant` means the authenticated owner view. Logs, traces, audit, analytics, and DLQs receive only fields explicitly permitted there. `Owner policy` means the retention of the authoritative owner described by the [Security Model](../architecture/SECURITY_MODEL.md); transport copies follow Inbox/Outbox/DLQ limits and verified deletion.

Reason-code fields below are traceable service-to-service codes. They are prohibited from applicant responses, UI, and communications until `Q-008` approves exposure or translation.

No route path is fixed by this documentary baseline. Every operation has `Implementation status: not started` and uses `StandardProblem` for safe transport, authorization, validation, conflict, and dependency errors; a technical or dependency error is never a credit outcome.

| Alternative cardinality and objectives | `alternatives.maximumCount`, `alternatives.objectives[]` | Values under `policyVersion`; eligibility, objective order, and canonical-terms deduplication are fixed by `rulesetVersion` | Product policy §9; decision table §8; BUSINESS_RULES BR-CD-004, BR-CD-005 |
| Offer validity | `offer.validityDuration` | Exact duration under `policyVersion`; expiry behavior is fixed by `rulesetVersion` | Product policy §§2, 13; Credit Decisioning design §5.2 |
| OP/G priorities, terminal precedence, outcome/disposition semantics, and primary-reason selection | No additional data fields | Fixed by immutable `rulesetVersion`; implementations cannot override or infer these behaviors | BUSINESS_RULES BR-CD-002, BR-CD-003; decision table §§3–5, §14 |
| Payment-capacity, present-value, installment, annual-rate conversion, total-repayment, and canonical hashing algorithms | `calculation.formulaVersions[]` | Each algorithm and unit convention is fixed by its immutable formula version | Product policy §§6, 9–10; decision table §8; Credit Decisioning design §§6, 8 |
| Monetary rounding and formula precision | `calculation.roundingMode` plus `calculation.formulaVersions[]` | Mode is explicit; scale/operation-specific precision belongs to the referenced formula version | Decision table §2, §8; Credit Decisioning design §6 |
| Reason-code membership/classification | `reasonCodeCatalogVersion` | Internal catalog is immutable; `Q-008` still blocks applicant exposure/translation | Decision table §11; product policy §12 |
| Policy/rules/formula identities, activation, and integrity | `policyId`, `policyVersion`, `rulesetVersion`, `calculation.formulaVersions[]`, `effectiveFrom`, `checksum` | Published configuration and referenced versions are immutable | Product policy §15; decision table §14; BUSINESS_RULES BR-CD-001 |
| Localized applicant messages, production underwriting, jurisdictional values, provider thresholds, and raw fraud logic | Outside the initial contract | Prohibited or deferred; no implementation default is authorized | Product policy §§12, 16; Q-008; Security Model |

### 3.7 Evaluation-input ownership and transport

Application Process publishes the immutable upstream snapshot. Credit Decisioning validates that request, invokes its scoring port where required, normalizes only the explicitly assigned provider results, and then seals the complete `CreditAssessmentInputSnapshot` before evaluating a rule. The upstream `snapshotHash` continues to bind the request fields; Credit Decisioning persists a separate deterministic hash of the sealed snapshot containing the request plus normalized provider results. Neither hash permits mutable enrichment after evaluation begins.

| Contract/input | Rule IDs | Authoritative source | Transport/provider source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Contract validity and required fields | OP-001 | Contract catalog and AP request | `CreditAssessmentRequested.v1`; malformed input is rejected before aggregate creation |
| Consent references | OP-002 | CI consent, projected by AP | `payload.consentReferenceIds`; references only |
| Identity rejection before assessment | Not an assessment rule | CI | `CustomerIdentityRejected.v1` stops AP progression and never causes `CreditAssessmentRequested.v1` |
| Verified-identity transport precondition | OP-001 / request precondition | CI fact projected by AP | `payload.identityVerificationStatus` must equal `Verified`; any other value is rejected before aggregate creation and cannot become `Favorable` or `Unfavorable` |
| Identity evidence sufficiency after verification | OP-003 | CI | `payload.identityEvidenceStatus`; `Insufficient` means remediable credit-assessment evidence only and produces exclusively `PendingEvidence`, never identity rejection or `Unfavorable` |
| Income evidence sufficiency | OP-004 | Normalized scoring-provider evidence result | `IRiskEvidenceProvider` result incorporated into the sealed CD snapshot; no public provider contract or raw evidence |
| Provider availability/consistency | OP-005, OP-006 | CD adapter/runtime observation | `IRiskEvidenceProvider` typed failure/result; operational disposition only |
| Confirmed high-severity safety stop | G-001 | CI | `payload.safetyScreeningResult` through AP; no fraud details |

Supporting it requires a separately governed CI fact/request version and field allowlist; no initial implementation default is authorized |
| Application and maturity age | G-003, G-004 | CI-derived age through AP; CD term calculation | `payload.applicantAgeYears` plus requested/evaluated term and ruleset/formula semantics |
| Declared residence market | G-005 | CI declaration | `payload.residenceMarket` compared with `eligibility.supportedMarkets[]` |

| Verified income and obligations | G-010, G-011 | Verified financial snapshot through AP | `payload.verifiedNetMonthlyIncome` and `monthlyDebtObligations`; no raw evidence |

| Product, risk, affordability, segment, pricing, alternative, and reason behavior | G-003–G-014 | CD versioned policy | `QuickPersonalLoanPolicy.v1`, `rulesetVersion`, `formulaVersions[]`, and reason-code catalog; no hidden defaults |

No input is obtained by direct cross-context store access. An opaque ID is never treated as evidence. The scoring provider is assigned only normalized `riskScore`, income-evidence sufficiency, and provider-neutral behavior/evidence inputs needed by `RiskClassifier` and `CustomerSegmentClassifier`; it does not own product rules, credit outcomes, identity facts, active-application state, or active-loan state.

For the initial contract, G-002 coverage is deliberately limited to expiration after AP's verified/unexpired precondition check. The decision table's failed-identity and later-mismatch variants are not transport inputs in this workflow. This narrowing does not resolve identity renewal or reuse: `Q-006` remains open. `Q-008` also remains open.

## 4. Explicitly prohibited data

No initial contract may contain full names, addresses, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, government identifiers, biometrics, document bytes, raw identity or income evidence, bank credentials, destination credentials, OTP values or protected representations, secrets/tokens, provider credentials, raw provider request/response bodies, unrestricted fraud signals, internal rule traces, stack traces, or unbounded metadata maps.

## 6. Credit Decisioning validation scenarios

The future executable contracts must prove: a favorable decision with traceable `rulesetVersion` and offer; an unfavorable decision with internal traceable reason codes; `PendingEvidence`; `PendingRetry`; `OperationalException`; immutable offer creation; duplicate request/event handling; unsupported-version rejection/quarantine; and separation of malformed, provider, transport, and infrastructure failures from `Favorable` or `Unfavorable` outcomes. Applicant-facing reason evidence is excluded until `Q-008` is resolved.

| Scenario | Required input provenance | Governed evaluation | Coverage assertion |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| S-001 Happy path | AP request facts plus normalized provider score/behavior/evidence result | Policy risk/segment leaves, ruleset, formulas | Favorable; Preferred treatment, PTI, rate, cap, and all supported terms reproducible |
| S-002 New customer cap | AP request plus provider-neutral history indicating `New` | Segment leaves and ruleset classifier | Favorable; segment cap and rate determine result |
| S-003 Medium risk | AP income/obligations/request plus provider score | Medium band leaves and formulas | Favorable when calculated amount reaches product minimum |
| S-004 High risk | AP income/obligations/request plus provider score | High band cap/terms/rate and formulas | Only policy-permitted terms and cap are used |
| S-005 Score boundary 500 | Provider normalized score | Valid/eligible score leaves and closed band boundary | Evaluates as `High`, not below-threshold |
| S-006 Score boundary 499 | Provider normalized score | `risk.minimumEligibleScore` and G-012 ruleset behavior | Unfavorable with internal traceable reason; Q-008 still blocks exposure |
| S-007 No capacity | AP income/obligations plus provider score | PTI leaf and formula version | G-011 unfavorable; no technical-failure translation |
| S-008 Reduced offer | AP request/income/obligations plus provider score/segment facts | All caps and affordability formulas | Favorable amount is the minimum governed cap, not an invented default |
| S-009 Below minimum offer | Same sealed inputs as offer construction | Product minimum leaf, formulas, and G-013 | Unfavorable only when every permitted term remains below minimum |
| S-010 Missing identity evidence | CI `identityEvidenceStatus=Insufficient` through AP after verified identity | OP-003 ruleset behavior | `PendingEvidence` only; no identity rejection and no credit decision |
| S-011 Provider timeout | Typed scoring-port failure | OP-005 ruleset behavior | `PendingRetry`; no score fabrication and no credit decision |
| S-012 Fraud overrides score | CI `safetyScreeningResult` plus provider score | G-001 precedence under ruleset | Restricted confirmed stop wins without exposing fraud detail |
| S-013 Duplicate and low score | AP concurrent-application boolean plus provider score | G-008 precedence under ruleset | Active-application reason wins; lower-priority risk need not run |
| S-014 Preferred cannot override capacity | AP financial facts plus provider score/segment inputs | Affordability, segment, and precedence rules | G-011 wins; segment cannot override capacity |
